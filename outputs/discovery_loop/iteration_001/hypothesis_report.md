## 3. Underexplored Mechanisms

- **Thalamocortical Coupling and Spindle Generation**  
  - Current evidence links thalamocortical coupling to slow-wave generation and white matter integrity, but the role of thalamocortical coupling in spindle generation remains underexplored.

- **Thalamic Morphology and Beta Power in Relation to Sleep Stages**  
  - While thalamic morphology is linked to beta power via thalamocortical hyperarousal, the stage-specific effects (e.g., NREM vs. REM) are not well-explored.

- **Structural Connectivity and Insomnia Severity Metrics**  
  - Structural connectivity is linked to white matter integrity and insomnia severity (via ISI and PSQI), but the direct relationship between structural connectivity and insomnia severity metrics is underexplored.
